# Impact pathway: evidence and open links

[English](impact-pathway.md) | [简体中文](impact-pathway.zh-CN.md)

This is a claim map, not a forecast, causal result, or deployment promise.

| Link | Evidence owner and current evidence | Not established |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bounded execution → useful delivery | BAA and AIOS own the specified contracts and implementations. BAA records finite structural checks, isolated product acceptance, and mixed prospective results for named workloads in its [claim/evidence index](https://github.com/xiongweilin/BAA-Protocol/blob/main/experiments/claim-evidence-index.md). | Complete mediation in every deployment, general delegation gains, or production-wide safety. |
| Useful delivery → lower total human labor | BAA owns the task-level delivery and labor study design; the [claim/evidence index](https://github.com/xiongweilin/BAA-Protocol/blob/main/experiments/claim-evidence-index.md) marks total labor as unmeasured and links its current protocol status. | Net labor reduction across principals, reviewers, repairers, maintainers, and other affected roles. |
| Lower total labor → ordinary-user benefit | This depends on organizational allocation, access, and working conditions outside the execution protocol. | Who receives any saved time or whether it improves their outcomes. |
| Ordinary-user benefit → more discretionary time or life margin | This requires direct measurement with affected people and a suitable comparison. | Increased free time, autonomy, or quality of life. |

Current evidence reaches the first link only for specified interfaces and settings, with task-specific positive, null, and negative outcomes. The later links remain unmeasured. A passing contract check, completed task, or lower principal-attention count cannot stand in for total human labor or human benefit.

The strongest competing explanation for any apparent labor gain is burden transfer: less principal attention may be offset by review, repair, intervention, or maintenance work. If a suitable equal-input comparison finds no improvement in useful delivery or total human labor, remove the corresponding benefit claim while retaining narrower structural findings that remain supported.
