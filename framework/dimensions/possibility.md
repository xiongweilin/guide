# Possibility

[English](./possibility.md) | [简体中文](./possibility.zh-CN.md)

> Position: this dimension is a **working lens** for describing a purposeful finite actor's current boundary, not an irreducible ontological primitive. The six lenses prevent material semantic substitution; see [`../foundations/minimal-derivation.md`](../foundations/minimal-derivation.md) for the minimal derivation.

Possibility asks: **what else may occur, which candidates, futures, and paths remain open, which have been excluded, and on what grounds?**

Possibility is not the same as probability or capability. Probabilities, scenarios, branching futures, counterfactuals, reachability, and feasible sets are different ways to represent possibility structure.

## 1. Minimal states

At minimum preserve:

`unknown | possible | currently unreachable | currently reachable | excluded within an explicit scope`

Here, "unknown" is not "cannot."

`no path currently found != no path exists`

`cannot under current bounded conditions != permanently impossible`

`low probability != impossible`

`not in the current candidate set != excluded`

A strong claim of impossibility or current inability requires a sufficiently bounded candidate space and explicit grounds for exclusion.

## 2. Candidate space can be incomplete

A purposeful finite actor easily mistakes "the candidates already listed" for the whole possibility space.

At minimum distinguish:

- unknown values among known candidates;
- uncertainty among known candidates;
- candidates not yet generated;
- currently inaccessible but potentially important candidates;
- candidates that appear only after reframing the problem.

The possibility space itself can therefore become an object of exploration.

## 3. Probability and possibility

When the domain supports it, candidates may carry probabilities, confidence intervals, or other uncertainty measures. But:

`quantified probability != complete possibility space`

`high model probability != reality-side necessity`

`low model probability != safe to ignore severe irreversible consequences`

A probability model should expose its candidate space, data, assumptions, scope, and temporal conditions.

When the candidate space is incomplete, assigning precise probabilities to the known candidates does not eliminate unknown unknowns.

## 4. Reachability, controllability, and callability

For a purposeful finite actor, possibility further distinguishes:

- **possible**: a state has not been sufficiently excluded;
- **reachable**: a path exists from the current state;
- **controllable**: the system can exert enough control over key causal variables;
- **callable**: the necessary tools, interfaces, resources, collaborators, authority, and recovery paths are currently accessible.

`possible != reachable != controllable != callable`

Callable paths may arise through self capability, assistance, or leverage.

## 5. Current admissible space

Before decision, candidates can be excluded for different reasons:

- not a coherent action under the current framing;
- causally unsupported;
- currently known unreachable;
- required dependency or recovery path absent;
- sufficiency gate not met;
- outside current authority;
- violates applicable hard constraints, rights, contracts, safety boundaries, or commitments.

The remainder is the current admissible decision space.

Exclusion reasons must remain distinct:

`excluded because unreachable != excluded because unauthorized`

`excluded because of value conflict != excluded because causally ineffective`

If the set is empty, the system should not fabricate an option. It may change the goal, acquire capability or authority, renegotiate constraints, explore new candidates, or stop.

## 6. Future option value

A present action changes not only present state but also future possibility space.

Observe whether it:

- preserves alternatives;
- increases lock-in;
- permits switching tools and dependencies;
- preserves recovery and exit;
- preserves learning and redistinction;
- turns provisional commitment into irreversible structure.

Sometimes choosing a path that is not locally optimal but preserves materially useful future maneuverability is a real decision reason. It should not be turned into a universal optimization rule.

## 7. Possibility and exploration

Exploration does not mean enumerating every possibility. It means:

- finding missing candidates that could change the decision;
- generating competing framings;
- acquiring evidence that discriminates among important candidates;
- stopping provisionally when further exploration is not worth its cost.

`stop exploring != no unknowns remain`

`current boundary sufficient != possibility space complete`

## 8. Possibility and value

Risk is a crossing of possibility and value: whether an outcome may occur and how important that outcome is are different questions.

`may occur != worth pursuing`

`may cause severe loss != proven certain to occur`

How low-probability, high-impact, or hard-to-quantify events enter decision requires value, temporality, causality, and sufficiency together rather than allowing one dimension to own the conclusion.
