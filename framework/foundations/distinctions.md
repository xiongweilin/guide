# Nine Key Groups of Distinctions

English | [简体中文](distinctions.zh-CN.md)

These groups are **working resources**, not nine mutually exclusive entities, irreducible axioms, or an exhaustive classification. Apply the [operating principles](principles.md) according to the problem. Terms may overlap, contain one another, or be mutually exclusive in a specified sense.

## A. Understanding the world: what are we talking about?

| Group | Distinction | Confusion prevented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Representation, meaning, and object** | Equating words, signs, concepts, models, their meanings, and their referents; identical wording need not imply identical meaning |
| 2 | **Identity, classification, relation, and composition** | Conflating similarity, identity, category membership, association, part–whole structure, and organization |
| 3 | **State, event, and process** | Equating a state at a time, an occurrence, and change extended through time; ignoring scale and historical path |

See [reality](reality.md), [formation of distinctions](distinction.md), [relations](../topics/relation.md), and [temporality](../topics/temporality.md).

## B. Knowing the world: why believe it?

| Group | Distinction | Confusion prevented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4 | **Forms of knowledge and standards of warrant** | Conflating knowing facts, knowing how, firsthand experience, and explanatory understanding; imposing one test on all |
| 5 | **Observation, inference, evidence, and truth** | Treating measurement as infallible, inference as observation, or supporting evidence as certainty |
| 6 | **Logical form, strength of conclusion, and scope** | Treating valid inference, true premises, confirmation, and cross-context applicability as equivalent |
| 7 | **Description, prediction, explanation, causation, and intervention** | Substituting descriptive accuracy, successful prediction, adequate explanation, causal identification, and intervention effects |

Knowledge forms can overlap: skill need not imply accurate explanation; stating a rule need not imply skillful execution. Match verification to the claim via [principles](principles.md), [causality](../topics/causality.md), and [evaluation](../methods/evaluation.md).

## C. Choosing and acting: what should be done?

| Group | Distinction | Confusion prevented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | **Fact, value, norm, and authority** | Combining how things are, what is worthwhile, what ought to be done, and who may decide or execute |
| 9 | **Goal, means, conditions, decision, execution, and result** | Equating legitimate ends, effective means, satisfied conditions, justified choices, successful attempts, and observed results |

For actual action, further distinguish **possible, reachable, controllable, callable, and authorized**, as well as **proposal, commitment, admission, attempt, effect, verification, and completion**. Outcomes cannot retroactively rewrite ex ante grounds; local success does not entail aggregate success.

See [value](../topics/value.md), [possibility](../topics/possibility.md), [decision](../methods/decision.md), and [bounded action](../../applications/bounded-action/README.md).

## Application and revision

Ask which distinctions could materially change the current claim, then justify their meanings and basis. Use [system perspectives](systems.md) where fragmentation, cross-level inference, or missing relations matter. Strong counterexamples may warrant modifying, merging, or discarding a distinction. **A classification's existence is not proof of its empirical validity.**
